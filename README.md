# 🛡️ AASTU-CSC | CTF & Walkthrough Collection

Welcome to the official GitHub repository of the **AASTU Cybersecurity Club (AASTU-CSC)** — a dedicated space for storing **Capture The Flag (CTF) challenges**, **writeups**, and **detailed walkthroughs** used in our events and learning sessions.

This repository is designed to help students and cybersecurity enthusiasts at **Addis Ababa Science and Technology University (AASTU)** build their practical skills in ethical hacking, digital forensics, and real-world security problem solving.

---

## 🎯 About the Repository

This collection includes:

- **Custom CTF Challenges** developed by the club.
- **Writeups for TryHackMe, HackTheBox, and other platforms.**
- **Weekly exercises** used during our club’s training and workshop sessions.
- **Hands-on labs** covering key cybersecurity domains such as web, reverse engineering, binary exploitation, OSINT, and malware analysis.

The goal is to promote **skill development through practice**, and to create a reliable reference for members looking to level up their cybersecurity knowledge.

---

## 🏁 Events & CTF Hosting

The AASTU Cybersecurity Club organizes **weekly and monthly CTF events** for students at various skill levels:

- **Saturday Cyber Nights**: Regularly hosted capture-the-flag nights with real-world attack simulations and learning opportunities.
- **Themed Competitions**: Covering Web, Pwn, OSINT, Forensics, and more.
- **Workshop-based Challenges**: Tied to our on-campus training sessions and guided labs.

All challenges and solutions from past events are archived here to support continuous learning and future review.
